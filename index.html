How it fits fatatelier.com

The WordPress site stays. The platform plugs in beside it.

Marketing stays in WordPress

Home, About, Shop, Blog and the 360° tours keep running as they do now. New buttons on project and shop pages open the 3D tour or the studio.

Platform on its own subdomain

Something like design.fatatelier.com runs the 3D studio, accounts, submissions and tracking as a fast web app with its own database.

Real models in production

Tours are built from Fatatelier's own drawings or scans. Catalog pieces come from the team's CAD files, so customers configure what the shop can actually make.
